About

A Blueprint builds bridges between the golden era of funk and today’s electronic soul. Behind the name is a Swedish music producer and former DJ with roots in ’70s disco, ’80s fusion, and ’90s hip-hop. Each track is a blueprint — a groove constructed with precision, where the Rhodes and Clavinet carry the melody and rhythm becomes the architecture.. Released under Prime Arch Records.
